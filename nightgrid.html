FIELD CAPABILITIES

What he can do

Every ability draws from the same source — Meridian's power grid itself.

01

Arc Tether

Pulls a line of live current from any powered fixture and rides it — the way other people would use a rope, if the rope moved at three hundred feet per second.

02

Current Walk

Travels along active power lines and rail conduits undetected, reading the city's grid like a street map only he can see.

03

Grid Sight

Sees load spikes, dead circuits, and tampered lines from blocks away — usually the first sign that something's wrong before anyone calls it in.

04

Overload Pulse

A short, controlled discharge strong enough to drop a locked door or a hostile — and weak enough not to touch anyone standing near it.

CASE FILE 004-B · REDACTED

Grew up in the dark, literally

Sector 7 lost power for eleven days the winter he was fourteen — a "budget shortfall," the city said. He remembers exactly which apartments still had light and which didn't, and he's never stopped keeping that list.

A substation accident three years later should have killed him. Instead, it left him able to feel the grid the way most people feel a pulse — where it's strong, where it's failing, and where someone's about to cut it on purpose.

He doesn't fix the wiring. He shows up when someone tries to use the dark as cover.
